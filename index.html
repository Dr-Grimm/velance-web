Features

Everything you need to
own your focus

Velance combines OS-level tracking, rich analytics, and AI coaching into one lightweight desktop app that just runs.

Ambient Activity Tracking

No start/stop timers. Velance silently captures every app and browser tab you use and automatically classifies them as Productive, Supporting, Unclear, or Distracting in real time.

AI-Powered Focus Coaching

Powered by Gemini AI, Velance bundles your fatigue trends, focus scores, and peak hours into personalized coaching cards — warning you before burnout hits. Works offline too with a built-in rule-based fallback.

Rich Analytics Dashboard

Dynamic charts show your daily focus averages, distraction totals, and session counts. Peak-hours detection tells you exactly when your brain is at its sharpest — calculated from real behavior, not self-reporting.

Focus Sessions & Pomodoro

Structured Pomodoro-style sessions with live UI feedback. Every distraction during a session is logged so you can see exactly what broke your flow and how to protect it next time.

Tasks & Habit Tracking

Link daily tasks directly to long-term habits for hierarchical progress tracking. Habit heatmaps visualize streaks and target minutes over weeks, making it easy to spot consistency patterns.

Browser Extension Bridge

An optional Chrome and Edge extension gives Velance deeper context on your exact browser tabs — beyond just "Chrome is open" to knowing which specific website is stealing your attention.

Privacy First

Your data never leaves
without your permission.

Velance is built offline-first by design. All activity data is stored locally in a SQLite database on your own machine. The cloud is optional.

  • Stored locally on your PC Activity data lives in a local SQLite database — fast, private, and entirely yours.
  • Cloud sync is opt-in Supabase backup is entirely optional. You choose if and when your data leaves your machine.
  • Zero data selling, ever Velance has no ad model, no telemetry pipeline. Your habits are yours alone.
  • AI uses only aggregated metrics The Gemini AI coaching engine receives summarised scores and trends — never your raw activity log.
